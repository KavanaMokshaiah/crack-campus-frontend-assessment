FAQ Common questions about Crack The Campus Crack The Campus (CTC) is India's campus-to-career platform for engineering students and colleges — combining AI-proctored assessments, structured practice, CTC Score credentialing, and placement opportunities. What is Crack The Campus? Crack The Campus (CTC) is India's campus-to-career platform that helps engineering students prepare for placements through AI-proctored assessments, structured coding and aptitude practice, skill courses, contests, a recruiter-trusted CTC Score, and job opportunities. Who is Crack The Campus for? Crack The Campus is built for engineering students preparing for campus placements and internships, and for colleges that need secure assessments, student performance analytics, and placement-readiness tracking. What is the CTC Score? The CTC Score is a composite placement-readiness credential on Crack The Campus. It reflects a student's skills, practice consistency, assessment performance, and verified proctored test results — designed to help recruiters quickly identify job-ready candidates. How does Crack The Campus help with campus placements? Students practice aptitude, coding, and technical rounds through timed assessments that mirror real hiring patterns. Colleges can run secure proctored exams, track readiness dashboards, and students can build a CTC Score and apply to opportunities via Job Desk. Does Crack The Campus support aptitude and coding preparation? Yes. The platform covers aptitude, coding, technical preparation, structured learning modules in Skill Center, live contests, archived practice, and AI-guided learning — not coding alone. How are assessments proctored on Crack The Campus? High-stakes assessments run in the Crack The Campus desktop suite (Windows, macOS, Linux) with AI proctoring and integrity monitoring. This provides a secure, controlled environment similar to real company hiring tests. Is there a free plan on Crack The Campus? Yes. Crack The Campus offers a free student plan with no time limit. Students can explore courses, practice, and use core features without a credit card. Paid plans unlock additional premium capabilities. Can colleges and institutions use Crack The Campus? Yes. Institutions get an admin suite for assessment creation, batch management, AI-proctored exams, curriculum enrichment, placement analytics, and student engagement reporting. Visit crackthecampus.com/institution for details. How is Crack The Campus different from generic practice websites? Unlike standalone practice sites, CTC combines structured learning, timed proctored assessments, contests, recruiter-trusted scoring, institutional dashboards, and job-related outcomes in one campus-to-career ecosystem. Where can I download the Crack The Campus software? Download the desktop suite for Windows, macOS, or Linux at crackthecampus.com/download. The software is required for secure proctored assessments.
